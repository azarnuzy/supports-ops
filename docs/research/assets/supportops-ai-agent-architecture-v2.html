Decisions the model may return

REPLYCLARIFYESCALATERESOLVE
CLARIFY is capped at 2 per Ticket; the third becomes an Escalation. RESOLVE needs an explicit Customer confirmation.

Transport & isolation rules

No WebSocket — replies stream over SSE · WhatsApp webhooks are signature-verified · every query is Workspace-scoped · the AI Agent itself knows no Channel transport.

Knowledge conflicts

Two published Sources that disagree never get merged or ranked — the turn escalates with CONFLICTING_KNOWLEDGE and neither value is quoted to the Customer.
