# Getting started with SupportOps

**Visibility: Customer-Safe**

Creating a Workspace takes under five minutes. Sign up with a work email, name your Workspace, and you land straight on the dashboard — no credit card required for the 14-day trial.

During the trial you get full access to the Pro plan: unlimited Human Agent seats, the Web Widget, and AI Agent auto-resolution. After the trial ends, the Workspace automatically drops to the Starter plan limits unless you add a payment method from **Settings → Billing**.

To start receiving support conversations, connect at least one channel. The Web Embedded Widget is the fastest: copy the snippet from **Settings → Web Widget** and paste it before `</body>` on your site. WhatsApp is available as an additional channel once you link a WhatsApp Business number under the same settings page.

You do not need to configure the AI Agent before going live — it ships with sensible defaults and answers from whatever Knowledge Base articles you publish. You can always refine its instructions later from **Settings → AI**.
