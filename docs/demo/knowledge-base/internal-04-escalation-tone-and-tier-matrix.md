# Internal SOP: escalation tiers and tone

**Visibility: Internal-Only**

Not every escalation needs the same handling speed. Use this to triage the Shared Human Queue when several tickets are waiting.

- **Tier 1 — account access or security** (2FA recovery, suspected takeover, suspicious cancellation): claim within 15 minutes. These involve real risk of account loss and should never sit in the queue.
- **Tier 2 — billing disputes and refunds outside policy:** claim within 2 business hours. Confirm invoice/subscription state with the Business Tools before replying; never promise a refund outcome before that check.
- **Tier 3 — everything else the AI Agent could not resolve** (ambiguous requests, missing Knowledge coverage, repeated clarification failures): normal queue order, first response target per the Service Level article.

Tone: match the Customer's channel and language, stay concise, and do not restate the entire AI Agent transcript back to the Customer when taking over — a short handoff acknowledgement is enough, since the Customer already lived through the conversation.
