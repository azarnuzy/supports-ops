# Service level and uptime

**Visibility: Customer-Safe**

SupportOps targets 99.9% monthly uptime for the API, Platform, and Web Widget. Status and incident history are published on our status page; Admins can subscribe to updates by email or RSS from there.

Pro plan Workspaces get priority support: a first response within 4 business hours for anything escalated to a Human Agent, versus best-effort on Starter. The AI Agent itself responds in seconds regardless of plan, since it is not affected by support queue staffing.

Planned maintenance is announced on the status page at least 48 hours in advance and is scheduled outside 09:00–18:00 UTC where possible. Maintenance windows may briefly pause Web Widget message delivery; messages sent during that window are queued and delivered once the window ends, they are not lost.
